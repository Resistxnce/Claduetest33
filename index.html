How to play

  • Play a note: tap a string between two frets — it sounds that note (the thick low E is the bottom string, as in tablature). Tap a string before the nut for the open string. Several fingers can play several strings at once (multi-touch).
  • Strum: sweep your finger across the strings (before the nut for open strings or a held chord). Faster = louder.
  • All 24 frets: on small screens the neck is zoomed — drag the minimap (or press ←/→) to move along it.
  • Slide: drag a pressed finger along the string. Hammer-on / pull-off: add or lift a finger while the note rings.
  • Bend / vibrato: set "Dragging across strings" to Bends the string in Playing, then pull a held note sideways.
  • Release: notes ring out when you lift; turn on "Lifting a finger mutes the string" in Playing for fret-hand muting.
  • Controls: presets, amp knobs, pickup selector, tuning and capo live in the panel below the neck.
  • Keyboard: 1–6 pick strings (1 = high E), Space strum down, Shift+Space strum up, ←/→ move along the neck, hold M to palm mute, Esc mutes everything.
  • Learn: pick a chord to see the fingering, "Hold chord" to have it pressed for you, show scales and note positions.
  • Phone or iPad: turn the device sideways and tap Full screen for a bigger neck; use Add to Home Screen to run it like an app.
