How to play

  • Play a note: tap a string between two frets — it sounds that note (the thick low E is the bottom string, as in tablature; on an upright phone neck it is on the left). Tap a string before the nut for the open string. Several fingers can play several strings at once (multi-touch).
  • Strum: sweep the amber strum strip at the bridge end of the neck (or the zone before the nut): it picks the strings without fretting them, so it plays whatever your other fingers hold. Faster = louder. Tap one string there to pick it.
  • Barre chords: press a string and drag across the others in the same fret — the finger lies down across them like a real barre (a blue bar) and stays while you hold it; add the other fingers in front of it and strum the strip. Slide the bar along the neck to move the whole chord. On a phone turn on Hold in the toolbar: every finger stays down after you lift (amber ring), so you can build the shape one touch at a time; tap a held finger to let it go.
  • All 24 frets: on phones, tablets and small windows the neck is zoomed so every fret is big enough to hit — drag the minimap (or press ←/→) to move along it. Neck view in the panel switches between the whole neck and the zoomed one.
  • Slide: drag a pressed finger along the string. Hammer-on / pull-off: add or lift a finger while the note rings.
  • Bend / vibrato: set "Dragging across strings" to Bends the string in Playing, then pull a held note sideways.
  • Release: notes ring out when you lift; turn on "Lifting a finger mutes the string" in Playing for fret-hand muting.
  • Controls: presets, amp knobs, pickup selector, tuning and capo live in the panel below the neck.
  • Every fret from the keyboard: press Tab until the fretboard is focused, move the cursor with the arrow keys (Home/End jump to the open string / 24th fret) and press Enter to play; keep Enter held while moving to slide.
  • Keyboard: 1–6 pick strings (1 = high E), Space strum down, Shift+Space strum up, ←/→ move along the neck, hold M to palm mute, Esc mutes everything.
  • Learn: pick a chord to see the fingering, "Hold chord" to have it pressed for you, show scales and note positions.
  • Phone or iPad: turn the device sideways and tap Full screen for a bigger neck; use Add to Home Screen to run it like an app.
