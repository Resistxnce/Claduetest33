How to play

  • Fret a note: press a string between two frets. Several fingers can hold several strings at once (multi-touch).
  • Pick / strum: tap a string over the pickups, or drag across the strings to strum. Faster = louder, closer to the bridge = brighter.
  • Slide: drag a pressed finger along the string. Hammer-on / pull-off: add or lift a finger while the note rings.
  • Release: lift your finger to mute the string (configurable in Playing).
  • Guitar controls: the volume and tone knobs and the pickup switch on the body are live; so is everything in the panel.
  • Keyboard: 1–6 pick strings (1 = high E), Space strum down, Shift+Space strum up, hold M to palm mute, Esc mutes everything.
  • Learn: pick a chord to see the fingering, "Hold chord" to have it pressed for you, show scales and note positions.
