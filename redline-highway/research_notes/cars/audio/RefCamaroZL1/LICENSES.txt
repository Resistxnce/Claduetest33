Reference recordings for RefCamaroZL1 (Chevrolet Camaro ZL1, LT4 supercharged 6.2 V8)
Downloaded 2026-11-13 from Wikimedia Commons. SHA-1 checked against the Commons API.

No freely licensed recording of a Camaro ZL1 itself was found. These two are the closest:
an LT4 engine in another GM car, and a sibling supercharged GM 6.2 V8.
